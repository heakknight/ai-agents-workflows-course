# Support Policies

## Refund policy

- One-time course purchases include a 30-day refund window from the purchase date.
- If 40% or less of the related course has been completed, a full refund may be granted when the customer asks within the window.
- If more than 40% and less than 70% has been completed, a partial refund may be granted depending on goodwill, history, and context.
- If 70% or more has been completed, refunds are not available except with an explicit operator exception.
- Subscriptions renew automatically. Customers are responsible for canceling before renewal.
- Refunds are not provided for subscription renewals outside the original purchase window.
- Refund eligibility may be denied or restricted if abuse is detected, including repeat refund patterns or chargebacks.

## Internal refund checklist

Before recommending or issuing a refund, verify:

- Exact account email or user ID.
- Purchase date and refund request date.
- Whether the charge is an initial purchase or a subscription renewal.
- Course progress percentage for the related enrollment.
- Transaction status, prior refunds, chargebacks, and abuse flags.
- Exact transaction ID to act on.

## Access and license model

- Content is licensed, not sold.
- Access is personal, non-transferable, and non-commercial.
- Lifetime access means continued availability on the Lumenpath platform subject to these policies; it is not ownership transfer.
- Account sharing, credential sharing, resale, and redistribution are prohibited.

## Billing documents

- Customers can usually access receipts from their Lumenpath billing history.
- Do not promise custom invoice formats, VAT treatment changes, or accounting reclassification without operator verification.
